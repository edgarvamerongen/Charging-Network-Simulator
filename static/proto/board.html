  • Keeps the brand warmth (cream paper) and the photography, but flat: no overlay gradients, no rounded hero cards.
  • Cost leads the result; numbers are the largest thing on screen. Best for client demos and the one-pager.
  • Risk: media strips eat ~190 px of rail height; the least “tight” of the three.

Palette (per theme)

  • 3 surfaces: canvas, panel, raised (popover)
  • 2 lines: hairline, field border
  • 2 inks: ink, muted
  • 1 accent: coral — primary action + live route
  • 2 states: danger, success — text/borders only, never decoration

Geometry

  • One radius token (0–4 px); pills and circles only for the units toggle and map dots
  • No card-in-card: sections divide with hairlines
  • Shadow only on floating layers (dropdown, popover, toast) — one level
  • No blur, gradient or glow anywhere

Type

  • Inter only, tabular numerals everywhere
  • Caps labels 11 px / 600 / .08 em for every section and stat
  • Body 13, titles 14–15 / 600, figures 18–28 / 600 with a 12 px unit
  • Hierarchy by weight and size — never by colour

Map furniture

  • Airports: neutral grey dots, 2–4 px, no rings
  • Route: 2 px coral, 4 px white casing; return dashed; over-range danger
  • NRG2FLY assets: 10 px navy squares
  • Popups: white, hairline, no blur
