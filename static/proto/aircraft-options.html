What it is. The selected aircraft as a wide row with a 96 px photo, then the catalog as a table: every airframe with seats, battery and range side by side, profiles nested under the selected row, filters as two segmented controls above the list. Trade-off: the least photographic; but you compare aircraft without clicking, and the list scales to a 20-airframe Notion catalog.
